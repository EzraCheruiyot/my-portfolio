The Challenge

A new B2B trade platform needed to acquire verified vendors across Africa — with limited budget, no existing audience, and a product most SMEs had never heard of. Standard lead-generation campaigns weren't working.

The Breakthrough

After testing three ad-set approaches, a traffic-to-signup-page model paired with aligned organic content delivered 132 vendor signups in 2 weeks from €49 in ad spend — a €0.37 cost per registration that became the foundation for all future scaling.

What Was Delivered
Full Q2 Growth Plan
3-month strategy with 11-week content calendar, ad framework, KPIs, and Q3 bridge.
Content–Ad Alignment
Every organic Friday post mirrored the active Meta Ad — same CTA, destination, language.
Vendor Activation Funnel
30-day email + content sequence moving signups from registration to active profile.
A/B Testing Discipline
3 concurrent ad approaches tested to isolate the winning mechanic before scaling budget.
