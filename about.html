About · The founder

The person behind
The Back Office

The Back Office is founded and run by one marketer who treats your growth like a system — and who leads with verified numbers, not claims.

I'm Ezra Cheruiyot — a Kenyan digital marketing strategist based in Nairobi. I combine the precision of a systems thinker with the instincts of a storyteller: the kind of person who builds a full quarterly growth plan with the same care I bring to a single well-crafted caption.

I built The Back Office around one belief: strategy before execution, always. I don't run campaigns to fill a calendar — I run them because they're the right move, at the right time, for the right reason, and I track every one until the data says something worth acting on.

That last part is the whole point of The Back Office. Most marketing stops at "looks good." I'm building a service that goes further — measuring attribution, testing deliberately, and proving the return. It's the discipline I'm deepening through ongoing analytics and data training, because the future of marketing belongs to people who can show what their work is worth.

The bigger why

"African businesses deserve marketing that's honest about results. I'm building toward a future where a small business in Nairobi can get the strategy, the craft, and the proof that used to be reserved for big budgets — delivered with the speed of AI and the judgment of a senior marketer."

That mission shapes every engagement. I don't just execute — I help founders understand the why behind what they're building. Because the right why changes everything that comes after it.

How I work with clients

Deliverables, not hours

I don't bill by the clock. Every engagement is built around clear deliverables with agreed deadlines — you know exactly what you're getting and when. Results are the only measure that matters.
